# Usability session — consent

Read this out loud before the session. Give the participant a copy. Tick and sign at the end; the observer keeps the signed copy with the timing sheet. [M18-S08-T03]

---

**What this is.** Growize is trying out the Growize Console with the people who will use it every day. You will do nine short tasks and then some free work on a practice copy of the app while one observer watches and times you. It takes about 45 minutes.

**It tests the app, not you.** There are no right or wrong answers. If something is hard to find, that is a fault in the app, and it helps us to know.

**What is practice and what is real.** You will use the practice copy (the sandbox) with made-up investors. Nothing you do reaches a real investor, a real lead or a real bank. Please do not type a real investor's name, number, PAN or bank detail. If one shows on the screen, tell the observer at once.

**What we write down.** The observer notes how long each task took, where you hesitated or pressed the wrong thing, and what you said while you worked. Your screen may be watched live. We do not record your voice or video. The notes use "IR1" and "IR2", not your name. The key that says who is who stays with the observer.

**Who sees the notes.** The observer and Sahil (Digital Infrastructure), and the people who fix the app, as "IR1 found X hard". Your manager is not given your times or your name against them. The notes are kept with the UAT pack until go-live and removed afterwards [retention: Sahil to confirm].

**Your choices.** Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time, skip a task, or ask for a break, and nothing changes in how your work is judged. You can ask for your notes to be removed before go-live.

**Questions.** Ask the observer now or at any time, or write to Sahil.
