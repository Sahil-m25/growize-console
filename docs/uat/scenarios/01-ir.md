# UAT-IR — Investor Relations (IR)

- **Run by:** Rohit Verma (Kavya, Nikhil or Ananya may run it). The real seat holder runs it, not a stand-in.
- **Second person:** Kavya, as the second IR, for the refused checks.
- **What this proves:** One IR works a real day: capture a lead, follow up, NDA, said yes, supplementary draft, report the money, and see the paper and the allotment come back. The IR never sees money or identity. Two threads run: Thread A is a new lead 'UAT Lead A' you create; Thread B is the seeded investor Prakash Bhat, whose lead Rohit owns.
- **Before you start:** Sandbox seeded. Rohit signed in once. A farm with free units exists (Farms page, free > 0). Finance, Head of Finance and Head of AM testers on call for the hand-offs marked BATON.
- **Hand-offs:** BATON out: IR-05 (next step 'Send the NDA'), IR-08 (says NDA signed), IR-11 (said yes), IR-13 (final supplementary draft agreed), IR-14 (payment report). BATON in: IR-08 (FIN-06), IR-09 (FIN-08), IR-16 (FIN-11), IR-17 (HOF-07).
- **How to mark:** write Pass or Fail for every step in `../signoff-sheet.csv` (same step number). A Fail needs a defect (see `../defect-intake.md`). P1 = go-live blocker if it fails. P2 = needs a written workaround if it fails.
- **Labels:** "Refused check" means the seat must be denied. Passing it means you were stopped, with a sentence on the page and no browser pop-up.
