# Quick guide: IR Manager

Seat: IR Manager, Lead side. Dated 4 Oct 2026. Screenshots from staging go here when staging exists.

## Your job in one line
Run the team: see everyone's book, deal out new leads, cover for absences, read the numbers, and grant pages to your IRs. You see no money.

## Start of day
1. Sign in with Zoho.
2. Open **Leads**. The scope is your team's book. Use the **Owner** filter to see each IR's count. Lost leads are held back with a "Show" link.
3. Open **Needs an owner** and deal out unassigned leads.

## Your day
**Search.** Press Ctrl+K. You search your team's leads only. Up to 8 rows: name, last 4 digits of the phone, stage and owner. No investors.

**Add a lead with no owner.** Press **Add lead**, leave Owner blank. It joins **Needs an owner**. Press **Assign owner** and pick the IR.

**Load an event sheet.** Open **Events**, open a completed event with staff named, load the CSV. Read the preview: each row shows added or refused and why. Choose **Round-robin** and read the split before you load. Only good rows are written, with no consent recorded. Loading the same sheet twice adds nothing.

**Cover for a colleague.** Hand a lead to another IR for a number of days. They can open and work it and see "covering until <date>". They still cannot open other IRs' leads. When the cover ends, access ends. Cover cannot be forced on a lead whose owner is healthy.

**Numbers.** The first section is **Assignments by IR**: one row per IR and a team total, with This week, Last week, Earlier this month, Before this month, Total and Missed first touch. A lead assigned today counts as "Not yet worked" until the IR contacts it. Then Funnel, Checks (tap a figure to open the exact leads) and Transfers. No rupee amounts anywhere.

**Grant a page.** Open **Teams**, open an IR, grant a page such as Numbers. The IR sees it in their rail after signing in. You cannot grant System, change another manager's access, or make a loop.

**Updates and Activity.** Updates also shows leads with no owner, moves waiting for approval, and breaches in your team. Activity shows your team's actions, with a Person filter.

## What you will not see
- Money. Payment status is readable but there is no confirm or reject control.
- Payments, Farms, System, Investors-side Numbers. You get a short refusal.

## End of day
Sign out.

## If something goes wrong
- A save fails: press **Retry**. A conflict says "Changed by someone else, reload".
- Report problems with the route in `docs/launch/support-model.md`.
