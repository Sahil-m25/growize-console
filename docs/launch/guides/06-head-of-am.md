# Quick guide: Head of Account Management

Seat: Head of AM, Investors side. Dated 4 Oct 2026. Screenshots from staging go here when staging exists.

## Your job in one line
Give every investor a KAM and watch the whole book. You see no money.

## Start of day
1. Sign in with Zoho.
2. **Today** lists investors with nobody looking after them (for example "Tier B and nobody is looking after them") with **Assign manager**. There is no banked or outstanding figure.

## Your day
**Read the book.** Open **Investors**. You see all allotted accounts. Press **No manager** to list the ones without a KAM. There is no Paid, Due or KYC column.

**Name a manager.** Open the investor, press **Assign manager**. The list shows each KAM with their account count and how many have gone quiet. Pick one. The investor page then shows the manager and "Handed over: never introduced". The move is logged with both names.

**Assign a new investor.** When an IR's lead has said yes and the investor is allotted, assign a KAM. The KAM sees the investor at their next sign-in.

**Move an account.** Move an account from one KAM to another. The old KAM loses it on reload. The new KAM gets it with its history from lead to investor.

**Tickets and Numbers.** **Tickets** shows tickets in your team's scope. **Numbers** offers the **Service** section only.

**Updates.** In **Investor updates** you can publish **Produce** and **Notice** only. Statements and compliance updates are Finance's.

## What you will not see
- No Payments page. The investor page shows no Money and no Paper.
- **Teams** is read only. There is no seat control.
- No rupee amounts.

## End of day
Sign out.

## If something goes wrong
Report with `docs/launch/support-model.md`.
