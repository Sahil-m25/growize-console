# Quick guide: Key Account Manager (KAM)

Seat: KAM, Investors side. Dated 4 Oct 2026. Screenshots from staging go here when staging exists.

## Your job in one line
Look after your own investors: log conversations, answer tickets, hand bank and compliance tickets to Finance. You never see a rupee amount, a PAN, an Aadhaar reference or a bank number.

## Start of day
1. Sign in with Zoho.
2. **Today** reads "<your name>'s day" with your accounts and what is waiting on you. Tiles: gone quiet, accounts you hold, tickets open on you, conversations logged. There is no banked or outstanding figure.

## Your day
**Your accounts.** Open **Investors**. The heading is **My accounts**. You see exactly the investors assigned to you. There is no Paid, Due or KYC column. Other KAMs' investors and investors not yet allotted do not appear, in search or by address.

**Open an account.** Sections: Who they are, What they hold, Care, Journey, Tickets. There is no Money or Paper section. You can **Change their details** and **Log a conversation**. You cannot name a manager or move the account.

**Log a conversation.** Press **Log a conversation**, save. The "conversations logged" tile goes up on your next read.

**Tickets.** Open **Tickets**. You see your own tickets only. A request raised in the investor app arrives as a ticket owned by you. Press **Reply to the investor**, save. If the app does not confirm, it reads "Reply not delivered yet".

**Hand a bank ticket to Finance.** Open the ticket, press **Hand it to Finance**, add the new cancelled cheque. The row says it was handed to the Head of Finance and offers no Close. The cheque is filed on the investor as a personal document and you cannot open it.

**Updates.** In **Investor updates** you can publish **Produce** and **Notice** only. Statements and compliance updates are Finance's.

**Numbers.** Only the **Service** section. No rupee amounts.

**Activity.** Your own entries only.

## What you will not see
- Any rupee amount, PAN, Aadhaar reference or bank number, on any page or drawer. PAN, Aadhaar and bank read "Finance only". KYC reads only "clear" or "with Finance".
- Payments and Farms pages.
- Send or Verify on documents.
- A control to close a ticket that is not yours. It is refused on screen.
- The app-access card is read only. It says Finance controls app access.

If you ever see money or identity on a page, stop and tell Sahil at once. That is a serious problem. Do not copy it anywhere.

## End of day
Sign out.

## If something goes wrong
Report with `docs/launch/support-model.md`: page, time, record id. No investor names.

Source: UAT-KAM (KAM-01 to KAM-12).
