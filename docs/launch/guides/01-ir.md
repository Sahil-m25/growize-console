# Quick guide: Investor Relations (IR)

Seat: IR, Lead side. Dated 4 Oct 2026. Screenshots from staging go here when staging exists.

## Your job in one line
Keep every lead you own moving, from first call to "said yes", and tell Finance when money is on its way. You never touch money or identity numbers.

## Start of day
1. Open the console. Press **Sign in with Zoho** and use your own Zoho login.
2. You land on **Today**. It reads "Your follow-ups" and says how many are due now and how many are open.
3. Work the "due now" rows first.

## Your day
**Add a lead.** Press **Add lead**. Enter name, a ten-digit mobile and the source. Tick the channels the person agreed to (WhatsApp, email, call) and say how they agreed. For source "Events", pick the event first. If the number already exists the form says so and offers to open that lead. If the number belongs to another IR you only see "Flag the duplicate to your manager".

**Log a contact.** On Today, press the call or message button. Answer one question at a time: what happened, then what is next. You never pick the channel again. The last tap saves. **Undo** stays for 10 seconds. After that it is saved.

**Papers (NDA, supplementary agreement).** The lead page shows a row for the paper. You tell the investor it is in their inbox and tap how you told them. Finance sends the paper through Zoho Sign. When the investor says it is signed, tap "They say it's signed". That is only your word. Finance checks the real signature. For the supplementary: upload your draft as a PDF, press **Draft sent**; upload a new version if it changes; press **Final draft agreed** when both sides agree. Then it is with Finance.

**Send the deck.** Only after the NDA is signed. Before that, "Deck follow-up" and "Webinar invite" are not offered. Press **Email**, choose the template, read the summary line, press **Send**. Nothing goes until you press Send.

**Move the lead.** Press **Mark done** on each step. **Qualified** asks you to state the scorecard. **Investor said yes** asks you to confirm. After that the investor appears on the Investors page, and Finance gets the next job.

**Report a payment.** After the supplementary is verified, press **Report a payment**: kind, method, amount, today's date and reference. It shows "Waiting on Finance". Your stage does not move. You cannot send a second report while one waits. Amount 0 and future dates are refused. You cannot mark "Fully paid" yourself: Finance has to find the balance.

**See Finance's answer.** If Finance did not find the money, the lead page says "Finance did not find it" and gives the reason. Your words stay as you wrote them. When Finance and the Head of Finance clear the gate and allot, rung 7 (Allocated) ticks on your next read.

**Close a lead as lost.** Answer "Not interested", choose a reason. Re-open is always there. A lead with money in cannot be closed as lost.

## What you will not see
- Another IR's leads or investors. Search says "No lead matches in your book".
- Paid, due, PAN, Aadhaar or bank. They read masked.
- Pages: Numbers, Teams, Farms, Payments, System. You see a short refusal in the page.
- App access: read only. Finance controls it.

## End of day
Press **Sign out**. Your drafts and pending saves are cleared for the next person.

## If something goes wrong
- "Not saved yet": your text is kept. Press **Retry**. If it keeps failing, report it.
- "Changed by someone else, reload": reload the lead and do it again.
- Report with the route in `docs/launch/support-model.md`. Give the page, the time and the record id. No investor names.

Source: UAT-IR (IR-01 to IR-24).
