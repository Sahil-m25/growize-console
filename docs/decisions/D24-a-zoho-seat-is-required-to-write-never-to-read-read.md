# D24 — A Zoho seat is required to write, never to read — read-only staff hold a portal login and no CRM licence

_7 Sep 2026 · §4 · §9 · seats revised again_

Added 7 Sep 2026. Reads are served from the Supabase mirror, so a person who only looks never touches Zoho and needs no licence; a person who writes does, because the portal acts *as them* in Zoho and that is what puts their name on the record and keeps the seat honest under D1. **Viewers** — Jhalak, the founder, tech, marketing, the BU owner and operations — sign in to the portal with their own login and cost nothing. **Three guards, or the rule stops being true:** a viewer identity is refused by every write endpoint in the backend rather than by a hidden control, with a test for it; a viewer sees only the masked mirror and can never reveal an identity, because a reveal is a logged action behind step-up authentication and a real seat (D22); and a viewer login is a second identity to remove, so it joins the Zoho account on the leaver checklist. **Also settled:** workflows, blueprints, validation rules, approval processes and webhooks are org-level configuration — built once by an administrator and applied to every user automatically; adding a person is a profile, a role and their own consent at first sign-in. Rules and functions are authored under an administrator identity that never leaves, because Zoho automations owned by a deactivated user fail quietly (Appendix A, AU5).

**23 Sep 2026 (D53):** **Superseded for the console.** Every human holds a full Enterprise seat, viewers included. The rule 'a seat is required to write, never to read' no longer describes this system: everyone reads under their own identity.
