# D22 — Step-up authentication on eight authoritative actions; the bank statement is the second hand for ordinary receipts and an administrator is the second hand for money leaving; one seat per human inside Finance

_7 Sep 2026 · §14 · §20 · week 7 of the plan_

Added 7 Sep 2026. Finance is one working pair — an assistant and the Finance head. **Step-up:** revealing an identity or bank account, executing a refund, forfeiting a hold, approving a bank change, any post-lock reversal, an erasure and any export each require a fresh code before the action runs, on the account of the person holding that authority, logged whether it succeeds or fails; three failures lock the action and alert. **Maker-checker, restated:** with one Finance pair, recorder ≠ confirmer cannot hold inside Finance, so the second hand moves — the weekly bank statement confirms ordinary receipts (a stronger check than a colleague, since the bank has no opinion), and the tech lead or Pradeep approves the handful of actions that move money out. **Rejected:** two people sharing the Finance login with a step-up code separating them — the audit log would name neither, and a secret shared by two people who share an account is not a secret; it is also the licence-sharing problem D1 exists to avoid. The second seat is ₹2,400 a month and is what makes both the trail and the lock real.
