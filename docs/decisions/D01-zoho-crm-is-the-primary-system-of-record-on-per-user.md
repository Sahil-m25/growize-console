# D1 — Zoho CRM is the primary system of record, on per-user seats

_4 Sep 2026 · founder · confirmed by Sahil_

Founder’s direction, 4 Sep 2026. **Why:** the CRM is to be the staff’s working system; per-user seats make the skin legitimate under Zoho’s terms and let the portals act as the real person, so Zoho’s audit log and field-level security do the attribution and the identity wall. **Rejected:** Supabase primary with Zoho as an outbound copy (my recommendation of 3–4 Sep — simpler rules, one writer, no API budget; rejected because people will work in Zoho); one or two shared seats behind a skin (a breach of “a user licence may not be used by more than one individual”, and a termination risk over other people’s money).
