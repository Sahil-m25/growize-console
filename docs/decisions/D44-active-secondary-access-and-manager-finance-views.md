# D44 — Dormant secondary assignments and IR-manager Finance oversight

_16 Sep 2026 · owner clarification during console review; supersedes unconditional secondary visibility in D37/D42/D43 and D40's default IR-manager Finance denial_

An IR named as a secondary must not see that lead merely because the backup field names them. The primary owns the normal view. The secondary receives the lead, its history and D42's read-only Finance mirror when ownership actually moves to them, when an authorized primary/manager explicitly hands it to that recipient for a valid current cover window, or when the enabled primary is genuinely unavailable during a valid current roster absence and the named secondary is actively available to cover. A temporary handover is an actual delegation, not a dormant backup label. It can be valid independently of roster leave; a dormant secondary cannot start it themselves on a healthy primary.

Use one active-secondary predicate for personal books, record membership, work, named/watching state and secondary-derived team admission. Validate a distinct enabled internal IR secondary, an assigned enabled eligible primary, current secondary availability, and strict explicit absence start/back dates. Future leave, malformed/missing dates, an unknown or disabled primary and a departure record do not activate access. If explicit cover metadata exists, only its valid current named recipient qualifies; an expired, future, invalid or different-recipient cover does not silently fall back to automatic backup access. Automatic leave-based access closes on the first day back. Explicit temporary transfers close on cancellation/expiry; returning a primary through roster controls clears their cover. Ownership reassignment grants the new primary view and preserves history.

Apply this boundary to Today/Leads lists, search, phone/contact fields, Activity, updates, weekly totals, histories, notes, source payments/documents, copy status, references, reports/exports, profile counts, direct routes, retained drawer bodies and popup renders. Pending actions and asynchronous record-specific announcements or clipboard fallbacks recheck current access. Actual authenticated staff roles and reporting links determine authorization; a mutable display role or a borrowed page must not import another IR's book. A descendant's dormant secondary label cannot pull a healthy outside-primary lead into a manager's team book. Preserve explicit current cover and existing legitimate primary/manager reads. Pure formatting functions do not establish record permission.

The IR manager, actual enabled `conv` role, receives Payments and Documents **view** access by default for assigned records whose primary owner is themselves or in their genuine managed reporting chain, intersected with Leads visibility. Give the manager a holistic read-only team history view without admitting unassigned records or unrelated primary teams. Explicit page revocation still wins. Other business-viewer sensitive defaults and Channel Partner restrictions remain unchanged. Managers receive the same safe typed Finance field projection as IRs: masked bank references, Finance-only signing references and no internal Finance note payloads. Team history permission does not grant recordless organisation-wide financial totals, inventory breakdowns or leadless Finance audit entries.

The manager's genuine primary-owner reporting chain includes known inactive former members so their records remain reviewable and reassignable. This historical oversight does not activate their secondary IRs. Unknown owners do not match the chain; existing elevated business oversight also retains its authorized historical primary records.

Finance's Investor Management portal remains the sole document/payment writer. The manager's default read cannot send, upload, verify or alter source documents, record/reverse payments or change account/allocation consequences. D43's manual local copy recovery remains separate Ops integration authority; manager Finance oversight does not confer it.

Both console versions enforce these rules against local fixtures. The Investor Management prototype has no secondary-IR lead relationship requiring a parallel change. The demo mirror and copy projection remain unconnected to a live source, and the in-memory queue is not durable. Authenticated backend queries, field projections and write endpoints must enforce the same current assignment/absence/cover/team rules before real data is used. Source/VM checks and the application build do not certify backend authorization or rendered appearance.
