# D42 — IR read-only Finance history for held leads

_16 Sep 2026 · owner clarification during console review; supersedes D40's default IR denial of Payments and Documents and earlier console document-round writing_

[D44](D44-active-secondary-access-and-manager-finance-views.md) narrows the secondary relationship below: assignment alone grants no lead or Finance-history view; it requires actual transfer or active leave/authorized cover. D44 also grants the IR manager read-only Finance history for their assigned primary team.

IRs need Finance's recorded payment and document history to communicate accurately with the investors they handle. Give an enabled IR Payments and Documents view access by default. These views mirror the Investor Management portal's source records; the console does not become a second ledger, document editor, signing system or filing cabinet.

Read scope is the current primary owner, assigned secondary or valid live cover, intersected with current Leads view access and a real assigned owner. Retain history on closed and Lost records while that relationship remains. Unassigned pools, team-only visibility, previous ownership, expired cover and channel-partner attribution do not authorize the mirror. Handover, role changes, record-access revocation and cover expiry take effect on pages, drawers, histories, notifications, exports and reference reads immediately. Channel Partners do not acquire these sensitive defaults. Other roles retain D40's explicit, bounded sensitive permissions and temporary grants.

Every console role remains unable to send, upload, replace, sign, verify or alter source documents, record or reverse confirmed payments, or edit source account and filing consequences. Disable document-round draft, agreement, reminder and signature-status mutations as source writes, including direct handlers/reducer actions. IR contact follow-ups and unconfirmed investor payment reports remain separate lead-work records; they do not change Finance's ledger or document state and are not presented as confirmed Finance history.

Use stable source receipt/document IDs, explicit lead-to-ARL account links, recorded amounts and methods, and actual source sent/completion dates. Do not reconstruct receipt transactions or signature history from lead stages, payment claims or the latest aggregate balance. Display a missing source ID/date/history as missing. IR projections keep bank references masked, signature references Finance-only, and internal Finance free-form notes out of the mirror and audit exports; historical notes may contain superseded references that cannot be safely redacted from the current balance alone. Account and document record IDs remain available for communication. Default IR mirror access does not grant organisation-wide financial totals or inventory breakdowns.

The standalone prototype and Next.js port read a local demo projection of the three accounts explicitly linked by `INV.lead` in `portal/prototype/investor-management.html`. The shared fixture `console/src/domain/finance-mirror-demo.json` and the standalone inline projection preserve the portal's transaction IDs and document sent/completion dates. Existing unlinked sample records can show only the Finance history fields actually supplied; missing detail is not invented. The source prototype's latest document state is not a complete immutable event/version archive, and the UI does not offer source-file downloads.

No live portal connection was added. Before connecting real records, authenticated backend reads must enforce the same held-lead and field boundary, and a trusted source consumer must map typed, idempotent Finance updates by source IDs. Freshness must reflect confirmed upstream reads. Console principals must have no source-write endpoint; D41's local pending-save indicator is not proof of Finance synchronization or uptime.

[D43](D43-investor-copy-gate-and-manual-recovery.md) adds separate investor-copy status and guarded local integration tracking recovery. It does not grant a console writer for any Finance document, payment or source investor record.
