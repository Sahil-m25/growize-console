# D18 — Automation is catalogued (§21) and bounded by six honesty rules

_4 Sep 2026 · §21_

Added 4 Sep 2026. **Why:** the founder asked for anything that makes the work lighter; the catalogue lists what a rule can do on both portals and the seam. **The bounds:** logged with a rule id; system touches never count as the IR’s; nothing automated moves money, lapses, refunds, deletes or bypasses an approval; nothing automated carries identity or an instrument; every rule has an owner and an off switch; a failed rule alerts. **Order:** bank-credit matching, automatic verification of clean documents, request routing with SLAs, KYC through a provider, the allotment transition with KAM naming, digests; Cadences last.
