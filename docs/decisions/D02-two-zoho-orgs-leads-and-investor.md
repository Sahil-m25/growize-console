# D2 — Two Zoho orgs — Leads and Investor

_stated earlier · confirmed 4 Sep 2026_

**Why:** the lead population is ten times the investor population, must stay analysable whole, and must never hold identity data; separate orgs separate the seats, the profiles and the blast radius. **Cost:** the handover is code (no native cross-org conversion), admins hold two seats, two audit logs, two exports. **Rejected:** one org with profiles and field-level security (one seat per person, native Lead→Contact conversion) — simpler, rejected for population separation.

**23 Sep 2026 (D51):** Two orgs is now conditional. The owner intends one Enterprise org; D23's identity-wall test (T11) decides it. If the wall holds, this decision is superseded. Full record: `D51-one-enterprise-org-built-from-the-super-admin.md`.

**23 Sep 2026 (D52):** **Superseded.** One Enterprise org holds leads and investors. The identity wall is field-level security, Private default sharing and the role hierarchy, with record-level sharing for D44 cover windows only. Full record: `D52-one-enterprise-org-for-leads-and-investors.md`.
