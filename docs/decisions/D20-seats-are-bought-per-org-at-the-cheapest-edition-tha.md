# D20 — Seats are bought per org at the cheapest edition that carries the features that org actually uses — Professional for Leads if the trial confirms it, Enterprise for Investor, Team users for KAMs; Zoho One only if the company adopts the suite

_7 Sep 2026 · Appendix C · answers Q15, refines Q16_

Added 7 Sep 2026 on two questions: is there a cheaper plan, and should these users move to Zoho One. **Why:** the Investor org is pinned to Enterprise by two features and nothing else is — approval processes and custom functions are Enterprise-only, and §20’s six approvals and §22’s invariant guards need both; the Leads org uses one blueprint, about five custom modules, validation rules and webhooks, all of which Professional carries, so it is a ₹13,000-a-month question answered by a fifteen-minute trial (Zoho’s own pages disagree on whether Professional has field-level security and encryption — Appendix C). KAMs stay Team users at $9 annual, with the care email moved onto the team module because a Team user cannot send mail from an org module. **Rejected — Zoho One:** Flexible User pricing (≈₹3,500) is allowed for a single division and would work, but it costs ₹34,000 a month more than the mixed-edition plan for apps this design does not use, and “a user can be part of only one Zoho One account” means the second CRM org still needs its own subscription, so it simplifies nothing here. **The one case where it wins:** All Employee pricing (≈₹1,500) is cheaper than these CRM seats alone below roughly thirty-five people on payroll and gives everyone Mail, Books, Desk, WorkDrive and Analytics — a company decision, not a Growize one (Q28), and it would move `agresearchlabs.com` mail into Zoho One, which cannot be split with standalone Mail on one domain.

**22 Sep 2026 — Standard was considered and cannot be used; Q15 closes in Professional's favour.** Checked against Zoho's three published surfaces. **An edition is a property of the org, not of a user** — every licence in an org sits at that org's edition, so "most users on Standard and one on Enterprise" is not a thing that exists. What *can* vary inside one org is licence **type**: Team Users on any edition, and Lite Users on Enterprise and Ultimate only. That, not a mixed edition, is the lever for cheap read-only people. On **Standard** the design does not fit at all: no blueprint (so D5's rung ladder has nothing to enforce it), no validation rules (so B13's required source, residency and consent cannot be enforced in Zoho either), **no field-level security — which makes D13 impossible**, 10 custom fields per module against Professional's 155, one lookup field against five, no page layouts, concurrency 10 rather than 15, and credits capped at 100,000 rather than 3,000,000. The custom-field ceiling alone ends it: the lead record needs far more than ten. **Q15 is closed by the same check** — all three Zoho surfaces now agree that field-level security is present on Professional and absent on Standard, so the week-one trial D20 asked for is no longer needed to settle that question. Two things stay open: whether the edition's "Queries" allowance gates the COQL REST endpoint (Q32 — material, because every filtered list and every aggregate in the console is COQL), and whether the per-profile "Zoho CRM API Access" toggle is edition-gated, which Zoho does not publish at all.

**23 Sep 2026 (D51):** The Leads org's edition is **Enterprise**, production included — Professional is no longer the plan, and if T11 holds there is only one org, on Enterprise. Full record: `D51-one-enterprise-org-built-from-the-super-admin.md`.
