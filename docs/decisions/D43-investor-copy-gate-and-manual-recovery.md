# D43 — Investor copy at signed agreements and Finance-confirmed 10%, with manual recovery

_16 Sep 2026 · owner clarification during console review; updates D10/D11 copy timing and complements D42's single-writer Finance mirror_

[D44](D44-active-secondary-access-and-manager-finance-views.md) subsequently narrows secondary copy-status readers to actual active handling and grants the IR manager team Finance oversight. It does not expand manual copy administration.

Automatically copy a qualifying lead into Investor Management when the required NDA and supplementary agreement are signed and Finance has independently confirmed at least 10% of the agreed investment amount. Use current Finance source evidence, investment units and unit price, explicit lead/account links, actual signature dates and unique receipt/document IDs. A claimed payment, lead stage, console balance or locally marked paperwork is not evidence. Pending/unmatched and reversed receipts do not qualify; refunds reduce the confirmed amount and forfeits do not add credit. Invalid, conflicting or ambiguous evidence closes the gate. Additional regulatory documents remain separate from this initial copy gate; full funding, KYC, applicable FEMA checks and allotment have their own source rules.

This is a copy, not a move or closure. Preserve the original lead, lead ID, IR owner and channel-partner attribution. At the initial copy the lead team still handles the lead; Finance writes all source documents and payments in Investor Management. After lead closure Investor Management owns ongoing investor maintenance; the console retains the IR's permitted view and does not unlock closed lead editing. Copying alone neither allocates units nor makes a tentative account permanent. The existing early Contact needed for Finance paperwork is not removed by this decision.

Keep a manual copy fallback for authorized integration administration. It must use the same source gate and destination identity lookup as automation, be idempotent, refuse conflicting links, preserve all source and lead records and record the actual requesting principal. Console lead read access is not copy authority. In the current console model local recovery requires the actual enabled Ops principal, unborrowed `system.edit` and `xfer.view`, canonical lead visibility and both Payments and Documents read permissions. Sahil's lead view remains read-only: this recovery changes separate local integration tracking, not the lead, ledger, document store or portal lifecycle. Borrowed grants, displayed-role changes, partner attribution and channel-partner ownership cannot confer recovery authority.

IRs review copy status from their currently held lead under D42's primary/secondary/live-cover scope and actual lead/source read permissions. Channel Partners receive no sensitive copy default. Other viewers retain explicit bounded permissions; receipt/document provenance is withheld when its source page is revoked. Queue labels and drawer/URL reads must observe current record access. The copy register shows linked or tracked records rather than filling the screen with every unqualified lead.

Validate stored tracking against its canonical lead and source account before displaying it. Return an explicit field projection and only provenance IDs belonging to that account, so a corrupt link cannot expose another lead's account, receipts or documents. Live rows in the legacy register obey the same copy-reader boundary; elevated historical rows retain their explicit existing scope. Legacy console acknowledgement handlers are read-only and cannot manufacture a destination acknowledgement.

## Verified current prototype boundary

`portal/prototype/investor-management.html` already has three explicit `INV.lead` links: L6 to ARL-INV-0208, L7 to ARL-INV-0209 and L13 to ARL-INV-0210. All three contain the signed required agreements and qualifying Finance receipts. Its payment handler records against an existing investor; it does not create an investor from a lead. The 10% payment starts a tentative app account and hold, full payment makes that account permanent, and allotment is separate. The original IR stays on the source record while a later KAM is recorded separately. The portal's local INBOX/OUTBOX arrays are not an intake connection or delivery acknowledgement.

Both console versions use the shared three-account Finance demo projection, now including the source investment units and unit price. A private initialization builds separate automatic local copy tracking for these qualifying existing accounts. The manual fallback recovers missing local tracking using the same existing account ID; an existing entry is a no-op and a conflicting entry is refused. It does not mint an ARL ID, create a portal investor, write Finance records, modify the original lead, advance the lead or claim a remote acknowledgement. No artificial missing destination fixture is introduced. The interface says local demo and live intake not connected.

Manual local recovery uses D41's in-memory five-minute queue. It captures the principal/session, exact authority, canonical lead, source evidence and target copy link; it rechecks them before replay. Changes to even still-eligible evidence, investment terms, record identity or authority require fresh review. Offline recovery records no completed copy; expiry or a changed context fails with deliberate retry. Current read revocation hides private pending labels. This queue is neither durable nor an upstream Finance sync.

The source supplies document states rather than authoritative immutable version/current-version metadata. Multiple records for the same required agreement are conservatively ambiguous, even if an older record has been voided or a newer version is awaiting signature; live re-signing needs a trusted current-version marker instead of arbitrary selection. Malformed histories and duplicate source IDs are refused. The demo gate recognizes the known Finance producers captured in its trusted initial source snapshot; this fixture check is not authenticated live producer verification.

## Work required for live copying

A trusted authenticated backend must consume typed Finance evidence keyed to the lead, perform an idempotent destination lookup/create, acknowledge the actual destination ID and support automated retry plus authorized manual recovery. Declare any cross-system events in `contracts/` before emission. Enforce held-lead/source reads and recovery authority server-side, persist attempts and acknowledgements, reconcile reversals, and maintain the single-writer boundary after closure. The current fixtures cannot demonstrate an eligible lead whose destination account has not been created; that integration path remains unimplemented. Local source checks, inert-VM regressions and a Next.js build do not certify live copying, backend authorization or rendered appearance.
