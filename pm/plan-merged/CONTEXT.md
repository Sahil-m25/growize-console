# Context for rewriting the Growize plan as ONE plan for ONE app (25 Sep 2026)

## The product now
- Two plans existed: the IR console (lead side, epics E01–E17, stages S0–S5) and the Investment Management portal (Investors side, epics IM01–IM12, stages P1–P3). They are now **one app, the Growize Console**, one Next.js + TypeScript repo (D26), one sign-in, one rail, one Zoho CRM Enterprise org (D51–D54), zero copy: Zoho is the only store of business data (D45). Log planes per D47.
- The design is the IR console's (`console/prototype/ir-console-redesigned.html`); the reference prototype for the merged app is `console/prototype/growize-console-merged.html`. There is NO separate portal shell, NO slate-blue accent, NO "portal fence" and NO second design system any more.
- Rail bands and pages: **Leads** band (Today, Leads, Events, Updates-bell), **Investors** band (Investors, Farms, Tickets, Investor updates), **Money and paper** (Payments, Documents, Transfers), **Measure** (Numbers, Plan, Activity), **Admin** (Teams, System, Profile).
- Shared pages are ONE entry each with a "Lead side | Investors side" switch where both halves exist: Today, Activity, Numbers (lead Numbers + Investors Insights), System. Payments and Documents show only the fuller Investors ledger to a person holding both halves. Teams = member list plus an "Investors side seats" section.
- Each fact has one home. Leads is the book (ordered list, no action buttons); people act on Today. "Open the record" and "Assign manager" are the shared labels.
- Refusals and confirmations are in-page notes, never browser alert()/confirm(). Every write goes through commit() with a double-press guard and an honest "Not saved yet".
- **Seats:** IRs (4), IR Manager, Finance (Harsha — primary doer of every money and paperwork workflow), Account Management / KAM, Farm ops, Head of Finance, viewers, and **Sahil = Digital Infrastructure = super user (D68)**: every page and every action on both sides for testing; PII (PAN, Aadhaar, bank) stays masked; money and paper flows name Finance as primary doer, Sahil's drawer shows a "super user" note.
- Every human has their own Zoho seat and OAuth token (D53); cache keyed by visibility scope; field-level security + Private sharing + role hierarchy is the wall (D52); a restricted test user runs T11 before any second seat (D54). The org today has ONE user licence, paid until 13 Oct 2026.

## New decisions from the owner today (use them)
- **D69 — Search stays separate.** The top-bar search on the lead side searches leads only. Investor search lives on the Investors page. An IR never searches or sees investor data unless the investor came from that IR's own lead (belongs to them).
- **D70 — Documents and payments have three scopes.** (1) Personal, on the Contact: KYC, PAN proof, bank proof, nominee. (2) Per allotment (investor × farm LLP): NDA, supplementary agreement, allocation letter, unit certificate, payment proofs; payments are Receipts linked to the allotment (so to both the Contact and the LLP). (3) Project/farm, on the LLP: LLP deed, farm and progress reports, insurance, annual accounts — visible to every investor holding an allotment in that LLP and to staff with access to that LLP. The prototype's investor-only DOCS/TXN and "Block A–F" farms must map to LLP + allotment. ARL holdings/transactions stay a read-only panel.
- **D71 — Upload straight from the console.** Staff upload files in the console; the server streams them to Zoho (Attachments API on the scoped record, or ZFS upload + a file-upload field for typed slots such as "Signed NDA"); 20 MB per file; type allowlist; the user's own token so Zoho records who uploaded; no copy kept outside Zoho. Nobody needs the Zoho UI to upload.
- **D72 — Zoho Sign inside the console.** Finance (primary) sends a document for signature from the allotment or Contact: pick a Zoho Sign template or an uploaded PDF, recipients prefilled from the record, signing method (Aadhaar eSign / email OTP). The console shows status (sent, viewed, signed, declined, expired, recalled), reminders, recall, and the signed PDF. Zoho Sign webhooks (HMAC-verified) drive the status; on completion the signed PDF is attached to the allotment and `Agreement_Signed` is set. Investors sign by email link or embedded in the investor app (one-time URL, 2-minute validity, generated server-side only for the matching signed-in investor). The Zoho Sign request id lives on the Zoho record, not in a console database. Emails about a record are read through Zoho CRM's Emails API with the viewer's own token, so a person only sees emails of records they can open. Needs a Zoho Sign plan with API + webhooks (Enterprise or API plan; max 2 webhooks; India DC sign.zoho.in) — owner to confirm.
- **D73 — Tickets and updates go to the investor app.** Tickets (cases) and investor updates written in the console are pushed to the existing investor app (Supabase sign-in, D6) through the signed event contracts in `contracts/` (case.replied, farm.progress, push.delivered, request.raised…). The app's codebase will be added to the repo by the owner; until then the push is built against the contracts and a stub receiver.

## Timeline and delivery (D62, D65, D66)
- The autopilot builds progress-based, round the clock; target: everything built by **Sat 3 Oct 2026**, then a test-and-harden phase; go-live when gates pass (old calendar go-live 19 Oct). Zoho admin items come first in the queue.
- Merged stage order: **S0** Zoho org build-out and access wall → **S1** foundations, access, test suite → **S2** lead side daily work (capture, Today, Leads, lead page) + Investors pages → **S3** journey, gates, money, paper, Zoho Sign, farms/LLP → **S4** updates, tickets, app push, activity, numbers, teams, system → **S5** hardening, UAT, migration, release.
- Stories keep `plan` values: "In plan" (build now), "After go-live", "Dropped".

## Rules for a rewritten story
Same schema: `id, epic, title, as_a, i_want, so_that, acceptance[] (Given/When/Then, testable), priority (Must/Should/Could), stage (S0–S5), decisions[], prototype (page names in the merged console), zoho[] (exact modules/fields/APIs), depends_on[], plan`. Write for the one app: no "portal", no "console and portal", no second shell/fence/accent, no cross-app seams (a "seam" is now just two pages of one app), no duplicate pages. Name seats as above. Keep what the original was really for. Use plain words.
